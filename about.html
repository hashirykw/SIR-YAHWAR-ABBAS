Twenty years of making it click.

Sir Yawar Abbas teaches Cambridge O Level, IGCSE and A Level Physics in Karachi. He has taught A Level Physics at The Lyceum School since 2006 and coordinates the subject there, and he teaches batches at Prodigy Educations, Highbrow College, The Ambitions and The Classroom.

f. 02 — How he teaches

Four habits behind the method.

Prodigy Educations describes him as a Physics teacher known for simplifying tough, logical concepts and for mathematical modelling. In practice that looks like this.

01

The picture first

Every topic starts with what is physically happening: a diagram, a graph, a demonstration. The equation comes once the picture makes sense, so it is never just a formula to memorise.

02

Physics as a model

Students learn to turn a situation into equations and back again: what each symbol stands for, what a gradient or an area means, and when a model stops working.

03

Exam-oriented

Every class points at a paper: the command word, the units, the significant figures, and the line in the mark scheme where marks are usually dropped.

04

Practical skills, taught

A fifth of every grade comes from experimental skills. Tables, graphs, gradients, uncertainties and evaluation are practised as a routine, not left to the week before the exam.
