    The procedure

    From first message to exam day.

    Six steps, in order. Every student goes through the same ones, whichever campus they join or if they study online.

    1. 01WhatsApp

      Message his team

      Send your level, your school and the series you are sitting. The form above writes the message for you.

      • O Level 5054, IGCSE 0625, AS or A2 9702
      • May/June or Oct/Nov series
      • Campus or online
    2. 02Placement

      Get placed in a batch

      His team suggests the right batch for your level and series and shares the timings and the fee.

      • Batches at the academies he teaches at
      • Live online classes
      • Core or Extended advice for IGCSE
    3. 03Portal

      Receive your portal login

      Enrolled students get a student ID and an access code. One ID works on up to two devices.

      • Lectures filed by syllabus topic
      • Revision sessions
      • Announcements and test marks
    4. 04Syllabus

      Learn it topic by topic

      Work through the Cambridge syllabus in order, ticking each topic off in the tracker once it is secure.

      • 63 topics for 5054
      • 58 for 0625, 76 for 9702
      • Key terms and equations beside each one
    5. 05Practice

      Practise between classes

      Run the simulations in the Lab, drill the multiple choice, and log your scores on real past papers.

      • Nine Lab simulations
      • 46-question MCQ drill
      • Score tracker with a trend line
    6. 06Exam

      Sit the paper

      Walk in knowing each paper’s length, marks and command words, because you have already sat it against the clock.

      • Exam timer with focus mode
      • Mark schemes for every series
      • The equation sheet, level by level
    Questions

    Asked before enrolling.

    Not here? Ask Prism in the corner, or message the team.

    Where does Sir Yawar teach?

    He has taught A Level Physics at The Lyceum School since 2006, and teaches batches at Prodigy Educations, Highbrow College, The Ambitions and The Classroom in Karachi. Ask his team about online batches too.

    Which syllabuses does he teach?

    Cambridge O Level Physics 5054, Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 and Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics 9702, from AS Papers 1–3 to A Level Papers 4 and 5.

    Should I take IGCSE Core or Extended?

    Extended (Papers 2 and 4) opens grades A* to G; Core (Papers 1 and 3) is capped at C. Most students aiming for A Level take Extended. Ask his team if you are unsure.

    What are the fees and timings?

    They depend on your level, the academy and the batch, so Sir Yawar’s team quotes them directly. Send the form above and they will reply on WhatsApp.

    Can I see how he teaches first?

    Prodigy Educations has posted a demo class with Sir Yawar on its YouTube channel, and Highbrow College has a podcast episode with him. Ask his team about a trial class.

    How does the student portal work?

    Enrolled students receive a student ID and an access code. Signing in shows lectures and revision sessions for your level, announcements and your test marks. You can try the demo now with the sample student.

    Does he help with the practical paper?

    Yes. Readings, tables, graphs, gradients, uncertainties and evaluation are taught as a method for 5054 Paper 3 or 4, 0625 Paper 5 or 6, and 9702 Papers 3 and 5.

    Where do I find past papers?

    On the past papers page: 5054, 0625 and 9702 series in the current format, with mark schemes, a score tracker and an exam timer.
