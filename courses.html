What the course covers

The practical papers

Twenty per cent, taught as a method.

5054 Paper 3 or 4, 0625 Paper 5 or 6, and 9702 Papers 3 and 5 all mark the same habits. Sir Yawar teaches them step by step, so the practical paper becomes the most predictable part of the exam.

Ask about practicals
01Readings & tables

Repeat, average, consistent decimal places, units in the column heading.

02Graphs & gradients

Sensible scales, best-fit lines, a large triangle for the gradient.

03Uncertainty & evaluation

Sources of error and real improvements, not “be more careful”.
